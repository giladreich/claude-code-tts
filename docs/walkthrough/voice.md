# Get a voice worth listening to

The voice your computer came with needs no setup, and it is the one that sounds like a computer. Everything below runs locally too, and sounds like a person.

Run **Claude Code TTS: Set Up the Best Voice**. It picks the engine that suits what you listen to, asks once before installing anything, and tells you what it will download. Afterwards it offers to make you a voice of your own.

- **Qwen3** is what it installs for most people: the most natural voice here, and the only one that can speak as a voice you record or design.
- **Chatterbox** is what it installs when you listen in a language Qwen3 does not speak. It covers 23, in a voice you create, and it is slower.
- **Kokoro** and **Piper** stay available under **Choose Voice Engine** if you want something lighter, at the cost of a voice of your own.

Then **Claude Code TTS: Select Voice** browses the voices of whichever engine you have, and speaks a sample as you move through the list.
